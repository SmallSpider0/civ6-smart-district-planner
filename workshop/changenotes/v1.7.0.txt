Changes since v1.6.5:
- The Several cities tab is no longer limited to 8 cities: ‘All cities’ next to the heading selects every city and site tack. Above 8, the cities are planned in turns, in groups of up to 4 neighbouring cities, while the other cities’ districts stay where they are as neighbours; ‘Improve’ goes round once more. More cities take longer.
- When two cities on the Several cities tab share a river, only one of them gets a Dam. Before, both could get one, while the game allows one Dam per river.
- With many wonders ticked or on a short screen, the Solve button on the One city and Several cities tabs no longer slides off the bottom of the screen. The settings (wonders, weights, switches) now scroll in their own box when they do not fit, and the Solve button and the results stay in the panel.
- Reach uses the range you have now: as Toronto’s suzerain, the area effects of the Industrial Zone and the Entertainment Complex (and of districts that replace them, such as the Hansa) reach 3 tiles further, and the planner counts that. The tooltip of the result says so, such as ‘Industrial Zone (range 9, 3 more from bonuses you have now)’. Only bonuses you already have count.
- The City sites tab lists the sites in the order to settle them: each time the best one that can be reached next, and the tacks are numbered in that order. A site that needs another new site to reach it comes after that site, even with a higher score. A luxury or strategic resource you do not have yet counts for the first site that gets it, so site scores can differ a little; the chosen sites and the total are the same.
- On the Several cities tab, when ‘Districts for:’ shows a city with its own list, a button beside it puts the city back on the shared list. Before, this was only the last entry of ‘Apply a template’.
- The City sites tab counts Housing only from buildings you have researched: the Granary needs Pottery, the Harbor’s Lighthouse Celestial Navigation, the Seaport Electricity. The tooltip lists the rest as ‘not counted until researched’. Before, coastal sites with a Harbor got the Lighthouse’s and the Seaport’s Housing at the start of the game, so their scores and the plans can change.
- Dense packing on the City sites tab keeps the number of new cities you chose (3 by default, up to 12) instead of jumping to 12; with it off the limit is 6. The switch is now called ‘Dense packing: new cities close together’. Settlers take many turns to build, so plan a few cities, found them, then plan again.
- On the Several cities tab, when a wonder needs a district next to it (such as the Colosseum next to an Entertainment Complex) and no city taking part has one, the planner now also tries giving the district to a city that would pick it from its candidates anyway, and keeps the better plan. Plans can change in this case and solving takes up to about twice as long. Also fixed: making room for a wonder could give a city more districts than Per city.

自 v1.6.5 以来的变化：
- 多城页不再限 8 座城：标题右侧的“全部城市”把全部城市和城址钉都选上。超过 8 座时按位置分成每组至多 4 座相邻的城轮流规划，其他城的区域留在原处当邻居；“继续优化”再轮一遍。城越多算得越久。
- 多城页两座城共用一条河时，只给其中一座放堤坝。以前可能两座城各放一个，游戏只允许每条河一个堤坝。
- 单城、多城页勾了很多奇观或屏幕较矮时，“求解”按钮不再被挤到屏幕下面：设置区（奇观、权重、开关）放不下时在自己的框里滚动，“求解”和结果留在面板里。
- “覆盖”按你现在的范围算：当了多伦多的宗主国，工业区、娱乐中心（含商业同业工会这类替代它们的特色区域）的范围效果多 3 格，规划时按多出来的范围算，结果的悬停写明，如“工业区（范围 9 格，其中 3 格来自你现有的加成）”。只算已有的加成。
- 选城址页的城址按建城的先后排列，城址钉的编号也按这个顺序：每次排上能接上的分最高的那个，要靠另一个新城址接上的排在它后面。还没有的奢侈或战略资源算在先拿到它的城址上，所以各城址的分可能略有不同；挑出的城址和总分不变。
- 多城页“区域清单”选了一座有自定清单的城时，旁边多一个返回按钮，点一下这座城就改回共用清单。以前只能在“套用模板”的最后一项里改。
- 选城址页的住房只算已研究到的建筑：粮仓要制陶术，港口的灯塔要天文导航，码头要电力，其余在悬停里写“还没研究、未计入”。以前开局时挨着海、计划了港口的城址住房分偏高，城址分和方案会变。
- 选城址页打开“密铺”时新城数量不再跳到 12，用你选的数（默认 3，最多 12）；关着时最多 6 座。开关改名为“密铺：新城挨着放”。开拓者要造很多回合，一次少放几座、建好后再选一次。
- 多城页选了要挨着某种区域的奇观（如罗马斗兽场要挨着娱乐中心），参与的城都没有这种区域时，规划器还会试着补给本来就会从候选里挑这种区域的城，两种方案留更好的。这种情况下方案会变，求解最多多花约一倍时间。另修了一处：为奇观腾地方时可能让某座城超出每城数量。
