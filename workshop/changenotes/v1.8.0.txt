Changes since v1.7.7:
- Result rows and their tooltips now mark disaster risk: tiles next to a volcano (active or dormant) can be pillaged by an eruption, Floodplains on a river without a Dam can be pillaged by a flood, and Coastal Lowland without a Flood Barrier shows how many meters it lies and at which sea level stage it floods. Tiles protected by a Dam, a Flood Barrier, the Great Bath, Egypt’s ability or Liang’s Reinforced Materials are not marked. The disaster risk line on City sites now counts tiles the same way. Only Gathering Storm has natural disasters; other rulesets mark nothing.
- New switch “Avoid Disaster Tiles” in the settings of One city and Several cities, off by default. When on, districts go on tiles safe from disasters first; when a city has no safe tile, the district still goes there and is marked, so no district is left out. A Dam in the same plan makes that river’s Floodplains safe. With it on, scores may be a little lower; with it off, plans are the same as before. Shown only in Gathering Storm.
- Tile yields from civilization and leader abilities (such as Russia’s +1 Production and +1 Faith on Tundra, Mali’s +4 Gold on Mines, the Inca’s +2 Production on Mountains, the Māori’s +1 Production on unimproved Woods) now count on tiles the city does not own yet and on new city sites; those that need an improvement count the improvement the plan assumes. Canada can build Farms on Tundra. City sites and district plans of these civilizations change.

自 v1.7.7 以来的变化：
- 结果行的明细和悬停里标出灾害风险：挨着火山（活跃或休眠）的格可能被喷发掠夺，没有堤坝的河的泛滥平原可能被洪水掠夺，没有拦洪坝的海岸低地写明几米低地、海平面涨到哪一阶段会被淹。有堤坝、拦洪坝、大浴场、埃及的特性或梁的“加固材料”保护的格不标。选城址页的“有灾害风险”按同样的判断数格。只有风云变幻有自然灾害，其他规则集不标。
- 单城页、多城页的设置里加了开关“避开灾害地块”，默认关。打开后区域先放在不会遭灾的格上，城里没有安全的格时照样放下并标出风险，不会因此少放区域；同一方案里规划的堤坝让那条河的泛滥平原算作安全。打开时分数可能低一些；关着时方案和以前一样。只在风云变幻里显示。
- 文明和领袖特性给地块的产出（如俄罗斯冻土 +1 生产力 +1 信仰值、马里矿山 +4 金币、印加山脉 +2 生产力、毛利未改良的森林 +1 生产力）现在也算进还不归城市的地块和新城址的地块，要求改良设施的按规划假设的改良计入；加拿大可以在冻土上建农场。这几个文明的城址和区域方案会变。
