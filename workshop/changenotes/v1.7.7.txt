Changes since v1.7.6:
- A pillaged improvement now counts in tile values with the yields it gives once repaired. Before, a pillaged Farm counted only its terrain’s yields, lower than the empty tile next to it where a Farm could go, so a district on it looked cheaper and plans leaned towards pillaged tiles. Repairing costs no Builder charge, so both tiles now count the same. With pillaged improvements in a city, the same settings may give different plans and a different order.
- “Reach” now counts the district’s own city too: an Industrial Zone, an Entertainment Complex and the like (and wonders such as the Colosseum) that cover the city they belong to count that city as well, and a city covered by several districts of the same type still counts once. Before, when several neighboring cities on Several cities each planned an Industrial Zone, they counted each other as “newly covered” and the score was too high; now each city counts once, as in the game. A result row’s “covers N more” and the list of cities in its tooltip include the own city. With Reach on, plans change: picking districts from the pool on One city and Several cities takes districts with a regional effect, such as the Industrial Zone, more often than before.

自 v1.7.6 以来的变化：
- 被掠夺的改良设施按修好后的产出算地块分。以前被掠夺的农场只按地形的产出记，比旁边能修农场的空地还低，区域放上去看起来损失更小，方案会偏向占被掠夺的格；修理不花建造者次数，现在两块地按同样的价值算。城里有被掠夺的改良时，同样的设置可能给出不同的方案和排序。
- “覆盖”把区域所在的城也算上：工业区、娱乐中心这类区域（和斗兽场这类奇观）覆盖到自己的城也记一座；一座城被几个同类区域覆盖只算一次。以前多城页几座相邻的城都规划工业区时，会互相算作“新覆盖”，覆盖分多算；现在每座城只算一份，和游戏里一样。结果行的“新覆盖 N 城”和悬停名单里也列出本城。打开覆盖时方案会变：单城页、多城页从候选里挑区域时，工业区这类有范围效果的区域比以前更容易被挑中。
