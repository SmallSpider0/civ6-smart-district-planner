Changes since v1.6.3:
- On the Several cities tab, when you choose a wonder that must be next to a district (such as the Colosseum) and no city has that district yet, the district added for it is now always placed next to the wonder. Before, the Entertainment Complex could go to one city while the Colosseum went next to another city’s Entertainment Complex: the added one had no wonder beside it, the result still said ‘next to Colosseum’, and it used up one of that city’s districts for nothing.
- Selecting a city on the map again and opening the panel switches the panel to that city’s One city tab again. Before, if you had last opened the panel on the same city and then changed the city or tab inside the panel, it reopened on that city and tab instead, as if it had not followed your selection.

自 v1.6.3 以来的变化：
- 多城页选了要挨着某种区域的奇观（如罗马斗兽场），又没有哪座城有这种区域时，为它补的区域现在一定挨着这个奇观。以前补的娱乐中心可能在一座城，斗兽场却放到另一座城的娱乐中心旁边：补的那个旁边没有奇观，结果里仍写“供罗马斗兽场相邻”，还白占了一个名额。
- 在地图上重新选中一座城再打开面板，面板又会切到这座城的单城页。以前如果上次打开面板时选的也是这座城、之后在面板里换过城市或页签，再打开时停在换过的城市和页签上，看起来像没跟着选中的城跳过去。
