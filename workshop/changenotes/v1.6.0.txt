Changes since v1.5.4:
- City sites: a “Dense packing: as many new cities as fit” switch (off by default). With it on, layouts are compared first by how many new cities fit, then by the total score. New cities defaults to 12 and can be changed; it is kept together with the farthest distance. Each site in the result has a tick box: Place site tacks places only the ticked ones, and Select all ticks them all. In 12 test scenes at the same farthest distance, at most 6 cities fit before and dense packing fitted 12 in each; each city scores about 7% lower on average, and solving takes about 1.6 times as long.
- City sites: sites are scored part by part: Districts, Tiles, Features, Housing, Luxuries, Strategics and Appeal. Each part is turned into yields per turn, a typical good site in that part gets 10 points, and the points are multiplied by the part’s own weight, so by default every part weighs about the same and Woods no longer decide which site wins. The weights are in two rows: the six yields and distance, then one weight per part (Districts and Tiles are new). Each result line lists the points per part, and its tooltip gives the yields per turn behind each part and what earns 10 points. Luxuries count the cities that get Amenities from them once the plan is done, and extra copies by the civilizations you could trade them to. Strategics are graded by the era that reveals them and by kind (the first tile of a new kind counts double). Clearing Woods and harvesting are spread over 50 turns (was 20). The distance cost of districts far from the City Center is part of Districts and is weighted by it: none with Districts at 0, double under the Districts first preset, so there districts sit closer to the City Center. The same settings give different sites: in 11 of 12 test scenes the chosen sites changed; sites with fresh water went from 3 to 9, new Luxury kinds from 11 to 14, new Strategic kinds from 18 to 26, and the best tiles plus district potential are 1.8% lower in total.
- One city and Several cities: each district’s own score can be seen. The first line of a district row’s tooltip gives its weighted adjacency, with the tiles it takes and its distance from the City Center in brackets, and Housing and reach when it has them, for example “This district: weighted adjacency +4 (tiles -2.5; distance -1)”. Several cities shows a subtotal line for each city. The summary’s tooltip adds the adjacency by yield (before and after the weights) and what is not in any district’s line. The plans themselves do not change.

自 v1.5.4 以来的变化：
- 选城址页加了“密铺：尽量多放新城”开关（默认关）：打开后先比放得下几座新城，城数一样再比总分。新城数量默认 12、可调，和最远距离一起记住。结果里每个城址前有勾选框，“打城址钉”只打勾上的，“全选”一次勾上全部。12 个测试场景里，同样的最远距离下平时最多放 6 座，密铺都放下了 12 座，每座城的分平均低约 7%，计算时间约 1.6 倍。
- 选城址页的城址分改成各项分开打分：区域、地块、地貌、住房、奢侈、战略、魅力。每一项先算出每回合相当于多少产出，达到好城址的典型水平记 10 分，再乘这一项的权重，默认各项分量差不多，不再由森林多少决定选哪座。权重分两行：六种产出和距离一行，各项一行（加了“区域”“地块”）。结果行列出各项几分，悬停写出每项背后的每回合产出和满 10 分要多少。奢侈资源按方案完成后能分到宜居度的城市数算，多出的份数按能卖给的文明数算；战略资源按揭示它的时代和种类分级（新种类的第一格算 2 倍）；砍树、收获的一次性产出按 50 回合摊开（原来 20）。区域离市中心远的扣分算在“区域”项里、乘区域权重：区域为 0 时不扣，“区域优先”时扣双倍，所以“区域优先”下区域离市中心更近。同样的设置会选出不同的城址：12 个测试场景里 11 个选出的城址变了，有淡水的城址从 3 座变成 9 座，新奢侈资源从 11 种变成 14 种，新战略资源从 18 种变成 26 种，最好的地块和区域潜力合计少 1.8%。
- 单城、多城页看得到每个区域自己的分：区域行悬停的第一行写它的加权相邻加成，括号里是它占掉的地块、离市中心的距离，有住房、覆盖时也写，如“本区域：加权相邻加成 +4（地块 -2.5；距离 -1）”。多城页每座城一行小计。汇总行的悬停多写相邻加成按产出分开（乘权重前后）和不在各区域里的部分。方案本身不变。
