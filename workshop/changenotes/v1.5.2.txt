Changes since v1.5.1:
- City sites: districts are now chosen with the same grid as the One city tab (Must / Pool / Off) and a Districts count (3 by default, up to 6). Must districts count for every site; each site fills the rest with its best districts from the pool. By default every specialty district is in the pool except the Preserve, Encampment and Aerodrome. Before, no district was ticked and sites were scored on tiles, water and resources only; now they are scored with 3 districts even if you change nothing, so the chosen sites can differ and solving takes a little longer. These settings are separate from the One city tab and kept in the save.
- The result line leads with the weighted adjacency, with tiles, distance, Housing and coverage in brackets, for example “Weighted adjacency: +17 (tiles -25; distance -3.5)”. The sum is no longer shown: late in the game the good tiles districts take often outweigh the adjacency, and a negative sum made good plans look bad. Plan buttons show each plan’s weighted adjacency; on the Several cities tab the tooltip says how much planning together gains over planning city by city.
- The Preserve is Off by default in the district grid (few civilizations want one); set it to Pool or Must to plan it. What its Grove and Sanctuary give nearby tiles now counts under Tiles instead of Housing: each such tile counts at the better of its best improvement or the Preserve’s yields, only if the city would work it, weighted by Tiles like other tiles (nothing when Tiles is 0). Only buildings you can build now count: no Sanctuary before Conservation. Plans with a Preserve can change.
- The Distance weight defaults to 0.5 instead of 0: with equal adjacency, districts sit closer to the City Center. The same settings can give different plans (52 of 84 test scenes changed, adjacency 0.6% lower on average). Saves that never changed the distance use 0.5; changed values are kept.
- Coverage no longer counts the city being planned (its own Industrial Zone always covers it, which only pushed the pool towards Industrial Zones and Entertainment Complexes); only other cities covered count.
- Housing is valued by growth: each point counts fully up to the larger of the population needed for the planned districts plus 1 and the current population plus 2, and half as much for each point after that. Larger cities count less, since each new citizen needs more food (no discount up to population 5, about 0.3 at 16); city site tacks and new sites count as population 1. A Neighborhood is worth more in a small city short of Housing and less in a large one; plans with Neighborhoods and the fresh-water score of sites can change.
- Several cities: a district limited to one per empire, such as the Government Plaza, shows as built and greyed out once any of your cities has it (finished or under way), with its city in the tooltip; it can no longer be set to Must, and the result no longer lists it as not placed.

自 v1.5.1 以来的变化：
- 选城址页的区域改成和单城页一样的区域格（必建 / 候选 / 不建）和“区域数量”（默认 3，最多 6）：必建的每个城址都算，其余由每个城址从候选里挑最好的补足。默认除保护区、军营、航空港外都是候选。以前默认一个区域都不勾，城址只按地块、水源和资源打分；现在不动设置也按 3 个区域估分，选出的城址可能和以前不同，求解也慢一些。这些设置和单城页分开，记在存档里。
- 结果汇总行先写加权相邻加成，括号里再写地块、距离、住房、覆盖，如“加权相邻加成：+17（地块 -25；距离 -3.5）”；不再显示各项之和（游戏后期区域占掉的好地块常比相邻加成大，和是负数，看着像方案很差）。方案按钮上写这套方案的加权相邻加成；多城页悬停写一起规划比逐城规划多多少。
- 保护区在区域格里默认“不建”（只有少数文明适合建），要建时点成候选或必建。古树林、避难所给周边地块的产出改算进“地块”，不再算在“住房”里：这些地块按“修最好的改良”和“拿保护区的产出”中高的算，城市会耕作才计入，乘“地块”权重；只算现在能建的建筑，没学“保护地球”时不算避难所。有保护区的方案会变。
- “距离”权重默认从 0 改成 0.5：相邻加成一样时，区域离市中心更近。同样的设置可能给出不同方案（84 个测试场景里 52 个变了，相邻加成平均少 0.6%）。存档里没改过距离的读档后用 0.5，改过的保持原值。
- 覆盖加分不再算正在规划的这座城自己，只算被覆盖的其他城市（以前这一分只会让候选偏向工业区、娱乐中心）。
- 住房按增长算分：到“建齐计划区域所需人口 + 1”和“现在人口 + 2”中较大的那个为止每点算满分，之后每多一点减半；人口越多的城打折越多（人口 5 及以下不打折，16 人口约 0.3）；城址钉和选城址页的新城按 1 人口算。住房紧的小城里社区更值钱，大城里更便宜：有社区的方案和城址的淡水分会变。
- 多城页：市政广场这类全帝国只能建一座的区域，你任何一座城建了（建完或在建）之后显示“已建”、变灰，悬停写明在哪座城；不能再设成“必建”，结果里也不再列为未放置。
