Changes since v1.6.4:
- The City sites tab’s tooltip shows the Housing split into what the water gives and what each building adds, such as ‘Housing 11 (water 5; once built: Granary +2, Lighthouse +3, Seaport +1)’. Before it only said ‘Housing 11’, which looked like what the new city has as soon as it is founded. Site scores are unchanged.
- A district added for a wonder (such as an Entertainment Complex for the Colosseum) now counts toward the city’s number of districts. Before, it was added even when the city’s built and Must districts already reached Per city, so the city ended up with more than that. Now it only goes to a city with room left; when every city taking part is full, the wonder is left out and the result says why: raise the number, or set that district to Must. The One city tab works the same way.
- Plans 2 and 3 on the One city and Several cities tabs say why they come after the plan before, such as ‘Ranked after Plan 1: places 1 fewer district or wonder’ or ‘Ranked after Plan 1: the adjacency plus the items in brackets is 2.5 lower’, in the tooltip and under the summary once selected. The button shows the weighted adjacency, while plans are ranked first by how many districts and wonders they place and then by the adjacency plus the items in brackets, so a later plan could show a higher number with no reason given. Plans 2 and 3 are now also kept in this order after tiles are swapped between plans; before, only the best one was moved to the front. The plans themselves are the same as before; only their order can change.

自 v1.6.4 以来的变化：
- 选城址页悬停里的住房分开写水源给的和每座建筑另加的，如“住房 11（水源 5，建好后另加：粮仓 +2, 灯塔 +3, 码头 +1）”。以前只写“住房 11”，看起来像新城一建好就有 11。城址分不变。
- 为奇观补的区域（如给罗马斗兽场补的娱乐中心）算进城市的区域数量。以前一座城的已建区域加必建区域已经到了“每城数量”时仍会再给它补一个，超出数量。现在只补给还没满的城；参与的城都满了时这个奇观不放，结果里写明原因，调高数量或把这个区域设为必建就能放。单城页也一样。
- 单城、多城页的方案 2、3 写明它为什么排在前一个方案后面，如“排在方案 1 后面：比它少放 1 个区域或奇观”“排在方案 1 后面：相邻加成和括号里各项之和比它低 2.5”，在悬停里和选中后的汇总行下面。方案按钮上写的是加权相邻加成，排序却先比放下的区域和奇观多少、再比相邻加成和括号里各项之和，以前后面的方案数字可能更高，看不出为什么。整理换格后方案 2、3 之间也按这个顺序排好；以前只把最好的提到第一。给出的几套方案和以前一样，只是顺序可能变。
