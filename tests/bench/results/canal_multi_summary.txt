# Canal probe results (2026-11-25)

Produced by tests/bench/probe_canal.lua and tests/bench/probe_radius.lua on the
game's own engine (CityManager.GetOperationTargets), then compared with what
SmartDistrictPlanner decides. The raw per-plot dumps stay on the machine that
produced them (one CSV per game, tests/bench/results/multi/); the seeds below
regenerate them.

## Rule fitted to the engine

A canal joins two sides. Sides are water (coast, ocean or lake), a city center,
or a canal already built; a river is not a side. The two sides must be two or
three edges apart (a 60 degree bend, or straight through). A straight passage
needs the four other edges to be land; a bend needs the edge between its ends
to be land and at least one land edge on its outer side. Two canals may not
touch, and the map is drawn for the city that owns the plot (three rings).

## Second round: 45 games

10 random maps and 35 games with `--leader LEADER_DIDO --seed 1025..1036` and
`--seed 1002..1024`, one capital each, all technologies, population 25.

| | count |
|---|---|
| land plots scanned | 1267 |
| plots the engine answered (the rest are inland cities, no canal target at all) | 465 |
| of those, DMT allows a canal too, so the rule is what decides | 337 |
| rule agrees with the engine | 333 (98.81%) |
| rule refuses a plot the engine allows | 0 |
| rule allows a plot the engine refuses | 4 |

The four:

| plot | water sides | note |
|---|---|---|
| seed1020 (48,25) | 0,1,3,4 | tip of a spit; the two ends are two different seas |
| seed1026 (10,26) | 3 | city center plus a two-tile lake |
| seed1026 (10,28) | 5 | city center plus the bay the city already touches |
| seed1032 (9,35) | 1,4,5 | two-tile lake on one side, sea on the other |

Proxies tried and rejected because each one refuses plots the engine allows:
"two ends in the same water body" (refuses 62,32 and 49,24), "straight wins,
no fallback to a bend" (refuses 62,32), "the whole outer side must be land"
(refuses 61,31 and 62,32). The real discriminator is the water body, which
Plot does not expose.

## Two canals may not touch

At seed1032 the capital had three canal targets: (8,33), (9,34) and (10,34),
with (8,33) one plot from (9,34) and two from (10,34). After building a canal
at (8,33) the engine kept (10,34) and dropped (9,34): the limit is adjacency,
not one canal per city.

## Fourth workable ring (probe_radius.lua)

With Workable 4th Ring (Steam 3220078106) installed, the same map gives
`GlobalParameters.CITY_MAX_BUY_PLOT_RANGE` = 4 (3 without the mod), and the
engine offers 27 campus plots of which 10 are on ring 4, the farthest ring
being 4. Details in docs/notes/issue-379-work-radius.md.
